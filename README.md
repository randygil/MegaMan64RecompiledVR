# MegaMan64RecompiledVR
 Recompilation of Mega Man 64, with a first person VR mode for Meta Quest (standalone, Android) and PC VR (OpenXR).

This is a fork of [MegaMan64Recompiled](https://github.com/MegaMan64Recomp/MegaMan64Recompiled). The VR mode puts you
in Mega Man's place: the buster is on your left hand, the special weapon on your right, and the headset drives the
camera. See [android/README.md](android/README.md) for how to install, play and build it.

Other additions over the original:
- Mouse camera and first person view on PC.
- Seams between terrain tiles are hidden at high resolutions.

A copy of the game is needed: the ROM is not included. This is a fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.
